# Core Rules

## Product direction

SDQuest is a dark JRPG for browser and mobile, with an FF-like battle presentation. The visual direction began as 16-bit pixel art and may evolve toward a 2.5D JRPG presentation. Visual assets must remain replaceable without rewriting gameplay.

## Party and battle presentation

- The starting party contains three characters.
- Additional characters can be recruited.
- Party members are displayed on the left side of battle presentation; enemies are displayed on the right.
- Presentation is not the source of truth for battle rules or state.

## World systems

- Reputation and rumors are persistent world systems that can influence progression and outcomes.
- Special dungeons may exist outside the seven main locations.
- Travel has a 10% random-event chance.

## Saving and failure

- Inns provide manual saving, with autosave support elsewhere in the game flow.
- Death and combat abandonment impose penalties. Exact penalty values and timing are not yet canon.
- A comedy death chance of 3% is part of the game rules.

## Content and asset boundaries

- Requested monster work must create or modify only the requested monster; do not add extra monsters by implication.
- Gameplay identifiers and data must not depend on a particular sprite, model, animation, or UI implementation.
