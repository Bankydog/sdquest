# World

## Main locations

The main progression contains seven locations, in this established order:

1. Starting town / hero village
2. Magic city
3. Sea / trading city
4. Mining city
5. Border city
6. Demon forest
7. Demon king castle

Special dungeons may be placed outside this sequence. Their placement and unlock conditions must be represented as world progression data, not embedded in UI or scene presentation.

## World progression systems

- Reputation records how groups or places regard the party.
- Rumors are discoverable world information and may affect available paths, encounters, or endings.
- Travel can trigger a random event with a 10% chance.
- Inns are the designated manual-save location, alongside autosave support.

Specific factions, routes, quest gates, and special-dungeon contents are not yet canon.
